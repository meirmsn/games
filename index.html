Game explorations

Small games made with beautiful art and sound that I didn't make (thank you, Kenney). I'm excited to be making them, and I hope each one gives you a few relaxing minutes.

  • Reef

    Eat the golden, green and pink fish, dodge the ones with marks, dive deeper.

  • Googly Faces

    Tap the silly faces and watch their eyes wobble.

  • Walk on Red

    He needs the loo, and it's across the street. Cross before the light turns green.

  • Pixel Leap

    Build a monster, then run and jump through worlds that each redraw it in their own style.

  • Shooting Gallery

    A fairground range, one tap per shot.

    Coming soon

  • Island Hop

    Sail, step ashore, grab a car: errands across the islands against the clock.

  • Runes

    Light every stone; each rune raises stones from the water or sinks them.

  • Orbit

    Fling a probe round the planets and catch the next one's orbit.
